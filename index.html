Client FAQ

Before You Hire Me

Quick answers for clients who want to understand the workflow, project files, revisions, and what to send before starting.

Ask a Question
How long does a 3D animation project usually take?

Small product or character shots can take a few days, while detailed scenes, simulations, or full animation packages may take one to several weeks depending on length, revisions, and render complexity.

What do I need to provide before starting?

Please send references, product images or models, character rigs if available, desired duration, style direction, deadline, and examples of the quality or mood you want.

Can you provide project files?

Yes, project files can be included when agreed before the project starts. Some course files and selected breakdown resources are also available through my Patreon.

Do you work with Blender and Unreal Engine?

Yes. My workflow includes Blender, Unreal Engine, Autodesk Maya, iClone, Character Creator, Cascadeur, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Photoshop depending on the project need.

How many revisions are included?

Revision count depends on the project package and scope. I usually confirm the revision plan before starting so expectations are clear from the beginning.

Can you make animation for ads, games, products, or social media?

Yes. I can create product animation, character shots, cinematic scene renders, game-style motion, short social clips, and polished visual presentations for brands or creators.
